            Stores the bitwise OR of two numbers in the A register

            Stores the bitwise XOR of two numbers in the A register

        NAND bzz_000_100 (Bitwise NAND)
            Stores the bitwise NAND of two numbers in the A register

        NOR  bzz_000_101 (Bitwise NOR)
            Stores the bitwise NOR of two numbers in the A register

        XNOR bzz_000_110 (Bitwise Exclusive NOR)
            Stores the bitwise XNOR of two numbers in the A register

        NOT  bzz_000_111 (Bitwise NOT)
            Inverts all bits in one number and stores it in the A register
